# EasyAdd Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

EasyAdd does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **No accounts and no tracking.** EasyAdd has no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising.
- **No network access.** All calculations happen on your device.
- **Data stays on your device.** Your settings, memory value and calculation history are saved only on your device so they are there the next time you open the app. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you have questions, open an issue at https://github.com/AnthonySalvatoreErcolano/EasyAdd2/issues.
